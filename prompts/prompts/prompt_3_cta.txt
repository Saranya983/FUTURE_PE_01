Write 3 call-to-action (CTA) sections for Brew & Bloom Café, Hyderabad.

CTA 1 — Visit Us (location-based)
CTA 2 — Book a Table (urgency-based, for weekend bookings)
CTA 3 — Stay Connected (newsletter/Instagram follow)

For each CTA:
- Heading (5–8 words)
- 1–2 sentence supporting copy (trust-building)
- Button text (2–4 words)
- Optional: a small trust signal (e.g. "Rated 4.8 ★ on Google", "No spam. Just good coffee news.")

Tone: Conversational, never pushy. Make readers WANT to act, not feel sold to.
