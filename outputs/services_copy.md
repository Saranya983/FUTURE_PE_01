# Services Page — Brew & Bloom Café

## 1. Specialty Coffee
Tagline: Sourced from farms. Perfected in your cup.
Every cup starts with single-origin beans, roasted in small batches and
brewed to your exact preference. From silky flat whites to bold cold brews,
we treat coffee seriously — but never snobbishly.
Why choose this: Our baristas don't just make coffee — they help you find YOUR coffee.

## 2. Homemade Bakes
Tagline: Out of the oven. Into your hands.
Our bakes change with the season — expect banana walnut loaves, cardamom
croissants, and dark chocolate cookies that disappear before noon.
Everything made fresh each morning. No preservatives. No shortcuts.
Why choose this: Regulars set phone reminders for when the croissants come out.

## 3. Light Meals
Tagline: Real food for real hunger.
Avocado grain bowls, grilled sandwiches stacked high, and warm soups that
actually taste homemade. Eat well and get back to your day without
the food coma.
Why choose this: Lunch here means you'll count down to coming back, not to dinner.

## 4. The Space
Tagline: Designed for people who need to think.
Soft light. Comfortable chairs. A playlist that never interrupts. Our
interiors are a deliberate antidote to the city's chaos — for working,
meeting, or simply breathing for an hour.
Why choose this: People come in for 30 minutes and stay three hours. You'll understand when you arrive.
